Scope: Procore, Buildertrend (incl. CoConstruct), Houzz Pro, JobTread, Jobber, ServiceTitan, Autodesk Construction Cloud/Build, Fieldwire (Hilti), Knowify, Contractor Foreman, plus adjacent interior-design tools. Research date: 2026-11-07. Method note: in this cloud session the vendor domains (buildertrend.com, helpcenter.buildertrend.net, pro.houzz.com, procore.com, businesswire.com) could not be fetched directly (network allow-list), so most facts below come from search-engine extracts of those pages; the extracts quote the pages, but exact UI wording should be re-verified before being hard-coded into a spec. ## KQ1. Pipeline stages from lead (受注前) to warranty (アフターケア): exact stage names and handoffs

### Takeaway
No platform ships a single rigid "lead-to-warranty" state machine; the common pattern is (a) a sales/lead object with its own stages that is *converted* into (b) a job/project object with a short lifecycle status (Buildertrend: Presale → Open → Warranty → Closed; Procore: Bidding → Pre-Construction → Course of Construction → Post-Construction → Warranty), with (c) finer-grained workflow objects (quotes, change orders, punch items, warranty claims, service appointments) carrying their own statuses. Field-service tools (Jobber, ServiceTitan) model it as a document chain Request → Quote → Job → Invoice → Payment with per-document statuses.

### Cited Findings

**Buildertrend (residential builders/remodelers; absorbed CoConstruct)**
- Job statuses are exactly four: **Presale, Open, Warranty, Closed**. "Presale" = the lead stage where the rough estimate, projected schedule and initial proposals are prepared; Presale jobs can already use estimates, bids, daily logs and to-dos during bidding, which separates unsold from sold jobs. "Warranty" = job complete but within warranty period so builder and client can submit warranty requests. "Closed" = either never moved past presale, or finished both Open and Warranty; closed jobs drop out of the default Jobs menu and are reopened by setting Status back to Open. — [Buildertrend Job Management help](https://buildertrend.com/help-article/job-management/)
- Sales side: leads live under **Sales → Lead Opportunities**; a Lead Opportunity is converted with the **"+ New Job"** dropdown; a website Contact Form can auto-create a Lead Opportunity and notify the sales team immediately; unlimited photos/videos/documents can be stored per lead. — [Lead Opportunities Overview](https://buildertrend.com/help-article/lead-opportunities-overview/); [Sales management settings](https://helpcenter.buildertrend.net/en/articles/9221000-sales-management-settings)
- Buildertrend does not publish a fixed list of *lead* status values; its glossary defines "Status" generically as "the stage an item is in, such as what phase a lead is in". A third-party HubSpot-integration analysis reports the sync collapses lead status to two values ("Unqualified"/"Bad timing" → "No Opportunity", everything else → "Open"). — [Buildertrend Glossary](https://buildertrend.com/help-article/buildertrend-glossary/); [RevOpsHQ HubSpot vs Buildertrend](https://revopshq.com/blog/hubspot-vs-buildertrend)
- Buildertrend markets the whole lifecycle "from the initial sale to the final walkthrough" on a dedicated product-workflow page; commercial pages list closeout tools for punch lists, project documentation and final communications. — [The full project lifecycle in Buildertrend, mapped out](https://buildertrend.com/product-workflow/); [Commercial construction software](https://buildertrend.com/?p=116252)
- Warranty module (the aftercare stage): Active Clients submit claims from their portal; subcontractors see assigned claims, share updates and enter scheduling details from their portal. Each claim has a **Classification** (type of warranty coverage), a **Service Coordinator** (internal owner), a **Follow-Up Date** ("a scheduled date to check on the status of the claim"), and service work is either **Log Completed Service** or **Schedule Service Appointment**; the appointment is **Assigned to** an internal user or subcontractor and displays each party's **acceptance status**; after the visit there are **Appointment Notes**, **Client Feedback**, and a **Builder Override** to enter feedback on the client's behalf. Warranty claims can be created/updated from the mobile app. — [Warranty Overview](https://buildertrend.com/help-article/warranty-overview/); [Warranty on mobile](https://helpcenter.buildertrend.net/en/articles/11463976-warranty-on-mobile); [Construction warranty software](https://buildertrend.com/project-management/construction-warranty/)
- CoConstruct wind-down (competitor-reported, verify with Buildertrend): no new projects can be started in CoConstruct from **April 1, 2027** and migrations to Buildertrend must begin by **June 30, 2027** (JobTread blog); Projul says customers can add projects through March 31, 2027. Migrated customers keep CoConstruct pricing for months 1–3 then move to a "matched Buildertrend package"; JobTread cites a $699/month entry floor. — [JobTread: Leaving CoConstruct](https://www.jobtread.com/blog/leaving-coconstruct-here-is-why-builders-are-choosing-jobtread); [Projul CoConstruct shutdown migration guide](https://projul.com/blog/coconstruct-shutdown-migration-guide/); [rfp.wiki Buildertrend vs CoConstruct](https://www.rfp.wiki/specialty-industries/construction-engineering/buildertrend/coconstruct)
- CoConstruct had a documented set of warranty statuses (page title "What does each warranty status mean?"); contents not retrievable in this session. — [CoConstruct warranty status page](https://www.coconstruct.com/learn-construction-software/what-does-each-warranty-status-mean-)

**Procore (commercial GC/owner/specialty; project-level, no sales CRM)**
- Default project stages: **Bidding, Pre-Construction, Course of Construction, Post-Construction, Warranty**. AU/UK accounts show "Tendering" instead of "Bidding"; some accounts also have a "Concept" stage. Companies can add custom stages; a custom stage can have bidding enabled but no other default stage can be designated as a bidding stage; to delete a stage, projects must first be transitioned to another stage. — [What are the default project stages in Procore?](https://support.procore.com/faq/what-are-the-default-project-stages-in-procore); [Add a Custom Project Stage](https://support.procore.com/products/online/user-guide/company-level/admin/tutorials/add-a-custom-project-stage)
- API stage categories: `pre_construction, course_of_construction, post_construction, completed, not_awarded, canceled`; a custom stage maps to one category. — [Procore developer docs: Working with Project Stages](https://procore.github.io/documentation/tutorial-project-stages)

**Houzz Pro (interior designers, remodelers, specialty contractors — most relevant to a design-build/fit-out firm)**
- Marketing page lists default lead stages **new → contacted → meeting → proposal → won**; stages can be created, reordered, renamed or hidden; help center says **New** and **Won** cannot be hidden, and built-in stages cannot be moved, deleted or renamed (only custom ones). — [Houzz Pro Sales CRM feature page](https://pro.houzz.com/for-pros/feature-sales-crm); [How to Customize Your Lead Stages](https://pro.houzz.com/pro-help/r/how-to-customize-your-lead-stages)
- Leads can be filtered by project type, lead source or **lead manager**; lead owners are assignable; the leads list shows icons for upcoming calendar meetings and **unread client messages**. — [How to Manage Your Leads](https://pro.houzz.com/pro-help/r/how-to-manage-your-leads)
- Houzz Pro CRM is positioned for designers to "land more clients and repeat business" (i.e., the CRM is also used post-project). — [Houzz Pro blog: interior designers use CRM](https://www.houzz.com/pro-learn/blog/interior-designers-use-houzz-pro-crm-to-land-more-clients)

**JobTread (residential/light-commercial GCs; strong on job costing)**
- The job dashboard contains **Job Status**, Key Financial Data, **Action Items** and Location custom fields (product update). Zapier templates show third parties mapping JobTread job statuses such as "appointment set" to CRM pipeline stages, and an April 2026 operations brief describes moving a HighLevel pipeline to "Estimate Sent" when a JobTread proposal is sent — i.e., many JobTread users keep sales stages in an external CRM and treat JobTread as the post-sale job system. — [JobTread product updates 12](https://www.jobtread.com/product-updates-12); [Zapier template: update job status to appointment set](https://zapier.com/templates/details/8cf5af8caf9b9aa7540cef489dabc47e09921b46650e37d96b8ebfc0bfd4c5d8); [Operations Strategy Brief April 2026](https://hljt.pages.dev/)
- Customer portal lets clients review selections, approve change orders, sign documents and pay invoices; external portal users (customers and vendors) are free and unlimited. — [JobTread features](https://www.jobtread.com/features); [JobTread vs Jobber](https://www.jobtread.com/blog/jobtread-vs-jobber-construction-project-management-vs-field-service-software)

**Jobber (home/field service; document-chain model)**
- Workflow: **Request (optional) → Quote → Job → Invoice → Payment**. Requests are "the first optional step", created internally or submitted by clients online, and are converted to a quote or a job. — [Request basics](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/request-basics/); [Converting a request to a quote or job](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/converting-a-request-to-a-quote-or-job/)
- Quotes have six statuses; documented ones include **Awaiting Response**, **Changes Requested**, **Approved**, **Converted**, and archived. When a client requests changes the quote is hidden from client hub until amended and resent (or marked back to Awaiting Response); a client signature moves Awaiting Response → Approved; "Convert to Job" sets the quote to **Converted**; a deposit or payment schedule can be required at approval and paid in client hub. — [Quote list page and key metrics](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/quote-list-page-and-key-metrics/); [Quote Approvals](https://help.getjobber.com/hc/en-us/articles/115012715008)
- Job-level statuses referenced in Work Settings include **action required**, **requires invoicing** and **late** (definitions not retrieved). — [Jobber Work Settings (help-center mirror)](https://prototype-help-center.pages.dev/hc/en-us/articles/115009737088)

**ServiceTitan (HVAC/plumbing/electrical, also construction/roofing editions)**
- Job statuses: **Scheduled, Dispatched, Working, Hold, Completed, Canceled**. Scheduled = all appointments scheduled (or one scheduled and one on hold); Dispatched = at least one tech dispatched and none working/done; Working = at least one tech working; Hold = all appointments on hold; Completed can be set early leaving unused appointments; Canceled applies to the whole job (individual appointments cannot be canceled). An older page uses "In Progress" instead of Dispatched/Working, and the API filter uses `InProgress`. — [Statuses and actions on jobs and appointments](https://help.servicetitan.com/how-to/statuses-actions); [Place an appointment on hold](https://help.servicetitan.com/how-to/job-hold); [ServiceTitan MCP jobs_get_list schema](https://glama.ai/mcp/servers/@JordanDalton/ServiceTitanMcpServer/tools/jobs_get_list)
- Pre-sale: inbound calls are classified as leads (calls ≥60 s or flagged), and **Unbooked** calls are surfaced by **Second Chance Leads** for follow-up (see KQ2). — [Phone integrations in ServiceTitan (construction)](https://help.servicetitan.com/construction/docs/phone-integrations-in-servicetitan.md)

**Autodesk Construction Cloud / Autodesk Build, Fieldwire, Knowify, Contractor Foreman**
- Autodesk Build and Fieldwire are project/field tools with no lead pipeline; their "last stages" are punch list/closeout/handover. Brinkman Construction (AU 2024 talk) created ~90% of its punch list "without typing" and started turnover tasks sooner with ACC; Glenveagh Properties used ACC as a central mobile hub for punch lists, photos and issue tracking at closeout. — [AU 2024: How We Transformed Our Closeout Process with ACC](https://www.autodesk.com/autodesk-university/ko/class/How-We-Transformed-Our-Closeout-Process-with-Autodesk-Construction-Cloud-2024); [Glenveagh closeout case study](https://www.casestudies.com/company/autodesk-construction-cloud/case-study/glenveagh-properties-saves-time-money-during-closeout-with-digital-construction-quality-management)
- Fieldwire core: plans, tasks, markups, issue tracking, punch list, forms, real-time collaboration for field crews. — [Fieldwire punch list app](https://www.fieldwire.com/lp/punch-list-app/); [Fieldwire blog: punch lists to profits](https://www.fieldwire.com/blog/punch-list-subcontractor-software/)
- Knowify: job costing/QuickBooks-centred; milestone tracking, progress reports, project billing; a **customer portal is only in the Enterprise tier** ("contact vendor"); Capterra 4.5/5 from 109 reviews. — [Knowify pricing (Capterra)](https://www.capterra.com/p/135791/Knowify/pricing/); [Knowify reviews (Capterra)](https://www.capterra.in/reviews/135791/knowify)
- Contractor Foreman: estimates, change orders, online payments, daily logs, client portal; entry plan listed at $49/month; positioned as the broad back-office option for small contractors. — [G2 Contractor Foreman features](https://www.g2.com/products/contractor-foreman/features); [SelectHub Fieldwire vs Contractor Foreman](https://www.selecthub.com/construction-management-software/fieldwire-vs-contractor-foreman/)

### Inferences
- A workable canonical model for the target app is a two-level state machine: **Lead stages** (New → Contacted → Meeting/Site visit → Proposal/Estimate sent → Won/Lost; Houzz Pro pattern) feeding a **Project status** (Presale → Open/Active → Handover → Warranty → Closed; Buildertrend/Procore pattern), with Procore-style sub-phases inside "Open" (Pre-Construction/design → Course of Construction → Post-Construction/closeout). Every platform keeps Won/Closed/Lost as non-deletable terminal states and allows custom intermediate stages.
- The design/estimating handoff is modelled as *object conversion* (Lead → Job; Request → Quote → Job), not as a stage change, so history and documents survive the handoff. Warranty is modelled as a separate claim object with its own owner (Service Coordinator), follow-up date, classification and sub-assignment/acceptance, which is the closest analogue to アフターケア.
- Field-service vendors tie customer-visible actions (approve, request changes, pay deposit) to status transitions, which is what makes the portal useful rather than passive.

### Gaps
- Buildertrend's native *lead* status values and Houzz Pro's full default stage list (incl. "Lost") could not be confirmed from the help pages (fetch blocked); only the marketing list new/contacted/meeting/proposal/won is cited.
- JobTread's actual job-status vocabulary and whether statuses are customizable were not found in public docs.
- Jobber's job-status definitions (Upcoming/Today/Late/Action Required/Requires Invoicing/Unscheduled) were referenced but not retrieved.
- CoConstruct's warranty status list page exists but its contents were not retrieved.
- No primary source found on Autodesk Build "project stage" naming; it has no sales pipeline.

## KQ2. Centralizing client communication and handling unanswered messages / overdue items

### Takeaway
All leading platforms centralize communication through a per-job client portal plus job-threaded messaging, and the most adoption-driving pattern (2024–2026) is *pushing* structured updates (daily logs, schedule, change orders, invoices) to clients, increasingly AI-drafted. Overdue/unanswered handling is mostly **object-level** (overdue RFI emails to the ball-in-court assignee, quote follow-up reminders N days after send, unread-message icons, "Unbooked call" lists) rather than a global SLA engine; explicit red/yellow/green at-risk indicators and manager escalation were not found as documented features in the residential/design tools.

### Cited Findings
- Buildertrend: a **Messages dashboard** shows all messages tied to a specific job; messaging is available on mobile. — [Messaging overview](https://helpcenter.buildertrend.net/en/articles/9682671-messaging-overview); [Messaging on mobile](https://helpcenter.buildertrend.net/en/articles/11427683-messaging-on-mobile)
- Buildertrend Customer Portal has **Pay Now** and **Action Items** sections (deposits were recently added to both), i.e., the portal surfaces what the client must do. — [Buildertrend public roadmap: recently launched](https://buildertrend-publicroadmap.launchnotes.io/board/copy-of-template-66); [Client Portal FAQs](https://buildertrend.com/help-article/client-portal-faqs/)
- Buildertrend **AI Client Updates** (press release June 7, 2025): the AI reads Daily Logs, Schedule items, Change Orders and Invoices to draft a weekly client summary that the builder edits and sends via the Client Portal; early users report a 97% reduction in time (≈6.5 min vs 30–60 min per update). Buildertrend's year-end roundup called it the largest time-saving release of 2025; a "Generate AI Summary" item appears in March 2025 product updates. — [97% Time Savings press release](https://buildertrend.com/press-releases/buildertrend-launches-ai-tool-for-97-faster-client-updates/); [Roadmap: keep clients informed with client updates](https://buildertrend-publicroadmap.launchnotes.io/board/keep-clients-informed-with-client-updates); [Buildertrend unveils top 2025 product updates](https://buildertrend.com/press-releases/buildertrend-unveils-top-2025-product-updates); [Product updates](https://buildertrend.com/product-updates/)
- Buildertrend schedule follow-through: per-person **conflict notifications** ("Notify me when…" with a threshold number of conflicting items, set on the sub/vendor contact card); schedule **confirmation requests** go to the sub's email and are tracked on the item's **Confirmations** tab where the sub can confirm or decline; **Notify Assigned Users** sends one-off alerts from List View. No native daily digest email was found; a third-party AI assistant (Carly) builds its own 6 p.m. summary from Buildertrend's per-item emails. — [Advanced Schedule Overview](https://buildertrend.com/help-article/advanced-schedule-overview/); [Carly: Buildertrend API blog](https://www.usecarly.com/blog/buildertrend-api/)
- Houzz Pro: the leads list shows an icon for **unread client messages** and upcoming meetings; the **Client Dashboard** gives clients their communication history and project details, plus daily logs and a visual schedule; a branded custom dashboard can be shared. — [How to Manage Your Leads](https://pro.houzz.com/pro-help/r/how-to-manage-your-leads); [Houzz Pro Client Dashboard](https://www.houzz.com.au/for-pros/client-dashboard)

- Jobber **quote follow-up automations**: up to **two automatic reminders** for quotes in "awaiting response" status, each set as "N days after the quote was sent", sent through the same channel (email/SMS) and at the same local time of day as the original quote; quotes can be auto-archived to keep the pipeline clean; available on Connect plan and up (sources disagree on plan). — [Automations](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/automations/); [Quote follow-ups](https://help.getjobber.com/hc/en-us/articles/24244124296471); [Jobber: let Jobber handle follow-ups](https://www.getjobber.com/?p=38549)

- Jobber **AI Receptionist** (GA Aug 25, 2025) answers calls and texts and books work; included in Plus, add-on for Grow (a third party cites $99/month, Dec 2025). — [PM Magazine: Jobber launches AI-powered Receptionist](https://www.pmmag.com/articles/106754-jobber-launches-ai-powered-receptionist); [DestinationCRM](https://www.destinationcrm.com/Articles/CRM-News/CRM-Across-the-Wire/Jobber-Launches-Receptionist-171139.aspx)
- ServiceTitan: phone integration gives real-time call monitoring, AI-generated call transcripts, summaries and sentiment analysis, and **Second Chance Leads** flags **Unbooked** calls for follow-up; Phones Pro bundles these. — [Phone integrations in ServiceTitan (construction)](https://help.servicetitan.com/construction/docs/phone-integrations-in-servicetitan.md); [ServiceTitan Phones Pro](https://www.servicetitan.com/features/phones-pro)
- ServiceTitan dispatch board has a documented icon/alert glossary (visual alerts per job/appointment). — [Dispatching icon and alert glossary](https://help.servicetitan.com/construction/docs/dispatching-icon-and-alert-glossary)
- Procore: when an open RFI passes its due date, Procore emails the **Ball in Court** assignee (release note 2018); the RFIs tool has an **Overdue** filter; the RFI email events/recipient roles are a configurable table; punch-list **batch emails** can group multiple updates including a list of overdue items. A third-party blog claims Procore "can send daily reminders for overdue items" but no official doc was found. — [RFI release notes 2018](https://support.procore.com/products/online/user-guide/project-level/rfi/release-notes/2018); [How do I control which emails are sent during the RFI process](https://support.procore.com/faq/how-do-i-control-which-emails-are-sent-by-procore-during-the-rfi-process); [About Punch List Emails](https://v2.support.procore.com/product-manuals/punch-list-project/tutorials/about-punch-list-emails); [FollowupCRM blog](https://www.followupcrm.com/blog/how-to-create-an-rfi-in-procore)
- JobTread testimonial on portal adoption: "zero complaints from customers when they use the JobTread customer portal"; Feb 18, 2025 update lets users hide the job dashboard's financial summary during screen-shares with customers/vendors. — [JobTread vs Buildertrend](https://www.jobtread.com/blog/jobtread-vs-buildertrend); [Hide Financial Summary on Job Dashboard](https://www.jobtread.com/product-updates/2025-02-18-hide-financial-summary-on-job-dashboard)
- Buildertrend's own customer-success chatbots gave customers back 10,305 hours in a year (Stevie Award entry). — [Stevie Awards entry](https://stevieawards.com/node/21328)

### Inferences
- The "unanswered customer message" problem is attacked indirectly: (1) make the client's next action explicit in the portal (Action Items/Pay Now; approve/request changes), (2) auto-remind on documents awaiting client response (Jobber: 2 reminders, N days, same channel), (3) surface unread/unbooked lists to staff (Houzz Pro unread icon; ServiceTitan Second Chance Leads), and (4) email the responsible person when an item is overdue (Procore ball-in-court). A small firm's app should combine these: per-thread "awaiting reply since" timestamps + SLA timers, an "unanswered > X hours" list by owner, and a Teams/LINE digest.
- Because no vendor documents a manager-escalation chain, this is a differentiator the target app can add cheaply (e.g., 24h → owner, 48h → manager, 72h → director), using the ball-in-court concept (one accountable person per open item).
- Weekly AI-drafted client updates (Buildertrend) are the clearest 2025 evidence that *proactive* summaries reduce inbound "what's happening?" messages; the same mechanism can drive a LINE push to customers.

### Gaps
- No documented red/yellow/green "at-risk" message indicator or manager escalation feature found in Buildertrend, Houzz Pro, JobTread or Jobber (may exist in UI but not in retrievable docs).
- Buildertrend notification settings/daily summary emails and the Houzz Pro notification model were not retrievable (fetch blocked; search budget exhausted).
- ServiceTitan "Second Chance Leads" mechanics (how a call is marked unbooked, who is alerted, reporting) were not retrieved in detail.

## KQ3. Subcontractor/vendor availability, resource scheduling, capacity and designer/PM workload visibility

### Takeaway
Only Procore ships a true workforce-capacity module (Resource Planning, ex-LaborChart) with availability/skill data and a capacity forecast; residential tools handle sub availability through *schedule confirmations and conflict alerts* per assignee (Buildertrend), and field-service tools through dispatch boards and AI dispatch recommendations (ServiceTitan). No platform studied publishes a designer/PM workload dashboard as a default feature.

### Cited Findings
- Procore acquired LaborChart (workforce software for specialty/self-performing contractors) in Oct 2021 and launched "Workforce Management" as an end-to-end solution in July 2022; Resource Planning offers "centralised scheduling and communication as well as real-time insight into availability and skill set"; vendor listing cites digital labor scheduling, workforce data management, people management, forecasting and analytics. — [Procore acquires LaborChart (BusinessWire)](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20211022005348/en/Procore-Acquires-LaborChart-to-Improve-Construction-Workforce-Management); [Procore launches Workforce Management](https://www.procore.com/press/procore-launches-workforce-management-a-new-end-to-end-solution-for-the-construction-industry); [What is the Resource Planning/LaborChart app?](https://en-gb.support.procore.com/faq/what-is-the-laborchart-app)
- The standalone LaborChart iOS and Resource Planning Android apps were retired from April 2025 and the functionality moved into the main Procore mobile app (beta). Procore also describes a **Resource Forecast** view ("see your capacity, not just rows of numbers") for planners asking whether a job can be staffed. — [same Procore FAQ](https://en-gb.support.procore.com/faq/what-is-the-laborchart-app); [Procore What's New: Resource forecast](https://www.procore.com/whats-new/resource-forecast-see-your-capacity-not-just-rows-of-numbers)
- Buildertrend: subs/vendors receive schedule **confirmation requests** by email and can confirm or decline availability; status is tracked on the Confirmations tab; per-person **conflict notifications** with a threshold count; past items don't trigger conflicts. — [Advanced Schedule Overview](https://buildertrend.com/help-article/advanced-schedule-overview/)
- Buildertrend warranty service appointments are assigned to an internal user or subcontractor and display each party's acceptance status (sub availability for aftercare visits). — [Warranty Overview](https://buildertrend.com/help-article/warranty-overview/)
- ServiceTitan Atlas launch (Sept 18, 2025) includes **real-time dispatch recommendations**, AI booking agents and interactive SMS scheduling. — [SiliconANGLE: ServiceTitan launches Atlas](https://siliconangle.com/2025/09/18/servicetitan-launches-atlas-ai-sidekick-automate-contractor-operations/)
- ServiceTitan **Technician Scorecards** (Dashboard → Technician Scorecards) report per business unit with tabs Overview, Lead Generation, Memberships, Productivity, Sales KPIs — i.e., per-person performance/productivity visibility. — [Dashboards and scorecards home](https://help.servicetitan.com/construction/docs/dashboards-and-scorecards-home.md)
- Houzz Pro: leads can be assigned a **lead manager/owner** and filtered by lead manager (the only per-person workload lens found). — [How to Manage Your Leads](https://pro.houzz.com/pro-help/r/how-to-manage-your-leads)
- Houzz Pro (Dec 9, 2024): generate a detailed schedule from any estimate/proposal (AI), which is how small design firms produce resource-loaded schedules quickly. — [Construction Dive press release: Houzz generates schedules from estimates](https://www.constructiondive.com/press-release/20241209-houzz-instantly-generates-project-schedules-from-estimates-using-artificial)
- JobTread: dashboards are customizable (charts, graphs, tables per KPI) and reports can be scheduled/shared; no default workload view documented. — [Construction Reporting & Dashboards](https://www.jobtread.com/features/reporting-and-dashboards)

### Inferences
- For a 30-person firm with 18 designers, a per-designer workload view (active projects by stage × upcoming milestones × open client items) is not something to copy from Buildertrend/Houzz; it should be modelled on ServiceTitan's per-technician scorecard and Procore's capacity forecast, but simplified: headcount-days by week vs. planned tasks.
- Sub/vendor availability is best handled the Buildertrend way for a small firm: send a confirmation request per schedule item, record confirm/decline with timestamp, and alert the PM on declines or on N conflicting assignments; ANDPAD already carries site schedules, so the app should read confirmation status rather than duplicate it.

### Gaps
- No documentation found on Buildertrend/Houzz Pro/JobTread default PM or designer workload dashboards.
- Procore Resource Planning pricing and whether it suits sub-50-person firms was not determined.
- Fieldwire manpower/crew-planning features were not retrieved.

## KQ4. Default dashboards and KPIs

### Takeaway
Field-service platforms (ServiceTitan, Jobber) ship opinionated default KPIs around the funnel (call booking rate, quote conversion rate, average ticket, projected income/aged receivables), while construction platforms (JobTread, Procore, Buildertrend) emphasize job-cost/margin (budget vs actual by phase, WIP, change orders) and let users build custom dashboards; Procore's published ROI metrics are schedule days saved and rework reduction.

### Cited Findings
- ServiceTitan dashboard definitions: **Call Booking Rate** = calls booked from leads ÷ leads, where a lead is a call ≥60 seconds or flagged as a lead; **Total Conversion Rate** = converted ÷ completed jobs, where a job is a "Converted Opportunity" when invoice subtotal ≥ the per-job-type *sold threshold*; **Opportunity Job Average** is the dashboard's "average ticket"; **Closed Average Sale** is a separate metric; definitions differ between reports (e.g., Technician Dashboard – Revenue vs Dispatch Pro Technician Performance). — [Dashboard business metrics](https://help.servicetitan.com/docs/dashboard-business-metrics.md); [Modular dashboard overall metrics](https://help.servicetitan.com/how-to/modular-dashboard-overall-metrics)

### Inferences
- Default KPI set for the target app, synthesized from the above: (sales) lead → meeting → proposal → won conversion and days-in-stage; proposal/quote conversion rate; (ops) budget vs actual gross margin per job by phase, change-order count/value, WIP; (closeout) open punch items and days-to-closeout; (aftercare) open warranty claims by age and follow-up date; (cash) projected income 7-day and aged receivables; (people) per-designer active projects and open client items.
- Jobber's note that conversion can exceed 100% is a modelling warning: define conversion on cohorts (quotes sent in period that later converted) to avoid misleading numbers.

## KQ6. Common complaints and failure modes

### Takeaway
Across review sites the recurring failure modes are: feature overload and steep learning curves (Buildertrend, Procore), poor field/sub adoption when the tool adds steps without benefit to the person entering data, duplicate entry across systems (owner vs contractor, app silos), billing/contract practices (Houzz Pro cancellation, Buildertrend/JobTread price changes), and data lock-in/export difficulty.

### Cited Findings
- Buildertrend: G2 average 4.2/5 across 180 reviews; users find the learning curve steep; Capterra 4.5 from ≈2,486 reviews (support 4.7). Japan): a key issue is "the number of data environments they use to collect, manage and share project data". — [Deloitte: state of digital adoption in construction](https://www.deloitte.com/au/en/services/economics/analysis/state-digital-adoption-construction-industry.html); [Autodesk/Deloitte 2025 report page](https://getconstructioncloud.autodesk.com/deloitte-state-of-digital-adoption-in-construction-report-2025)

### Inferences
- For an internal app that must coexist with ANDPAD, Teams and LINE, the dominant risk is *duplicate entry* (the same complaint that sinks owner-mandated systems). The app should be a system of record for the customer journey and status, and *read* site data from ANDPAD and conversations from Teams/LINE rather than ask staff to re-enter them.
- "Feature overload" complaints argue for a minimal stage/status model with few mandatory fields, mobile-first capture (photo/voice), and role-specific views (designer vs PM vs manager).
- Billing/lock-in complaints are irrelevant for an internal build but imply that exportability (CSV/API) should be a design requirement from day one.

### Inferences
- The AI features with proven, quantified payoff for small firms are (1) AI-drafted weekly client updates from structured records (Buildertrend, 97%), (2) call/chat summaries and sentiment with "unbooked/unanswered" detection (ServiceTitan, Jobber Receptionist), (3) schedule generation from an estimate (Houzz Pro), and (4) natural-language Q&A over project records (Autodesk, Procore Assist). Risk prediction (Procore RFI → schedule impact) and photo-to-issue (Autodesk) are 2025 betas/early releases.
- For a Teams+LINE-integrated app, the direct analogues are: summarize each LINE/Teams customer thread into a structured "requirements & open questions" record; flag threads with no staff reply beyond an SLA; generate a weekly client update from ANDPAD logs/photos and push it to LINE.
